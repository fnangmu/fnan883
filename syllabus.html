Course Description

This course is about efficient markets and empirical asset pricing. We start with reviewing asset pricing theories, asset pricing models, and return predictability. We then focus on cross-sectional asset pricing tests and review the standard methodologies proposed in the literature. We study tests of the CAPM and multi-factor models, including time-series regressions, cross-sectional regressions and GMM. Part of class time is also dedicated to the construction of factors and portfolios for testing. In the second part of the course, we review the applications of asset pricing models to mutual funds, hedge funds and event studies. We will complete the course with a brief review of behavioral finance.

The class will focus on in-class presentations by students, which will constitute 60% of the grade

Course Objectives

  • 1. Develop skills to work with data and conduct empirical analysis in asset pricing

  • 2. Gain the skills and tools to make professional presentations for an academic audience

  • 3. Be able to critically evaluate contributions to the discipline in working and published academic papers.
