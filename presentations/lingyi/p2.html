Paper
TitleAsset Growth and the Cross-Section of Stock Returns
AuthorsMichael J. Cooper, Huseyin Gulen, and Michael J. Schill
PublishedJournal of Finance 63(4) (2008), 1609–1651
DOI10.1111/j.1540-6261.2008.01370.x
Abstract

Cooper, Gulen, and Schill examine whether growth in a firm’s total assets predicts subsequent stock returns. Annual asset growth provides significant predictive information beyond familiar firm characteristics, including size, book-to-market, past returns, and accruals. The predictive relationship is also present among large firms.
