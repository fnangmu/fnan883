Paper
TitleChoosing Factors
AuthorsEugene F. Fama and Kenneth R. French
PublishedJournal of Financial Economics 128(2) (2018), 234–252
DOI10.1016/j.jfineco.2018.02.012
Abstract

Fama and French compare asset pricing models using the maximum squared Sharpe ratio attainable from their factors. They examine the CAPM, three-factor, five-factor, and six-factor models, and study how profitability definitions, factor return construction, and firm size affect the choice of factors.
