The Conditional CAPM Does Not Explain Asset-Pricing Anomalies

Presented by Lingyi Wei

Week 9, Fall 2026

Paper
TitleThe Conditional CAPM Does Not Explain Asset-Pricing Anomalies
AuthorsJonathan Lewellen and Stefan Nagel
PublishedJournal of Financial Economics 82(2) (2006), 289–314
DOI10.1016/j.jfineco.2005.05.012
Abstract

Lewellen and Nagel ask whether a conditional CAPM with time-varying betas can explain the book-to-market and momentum anomalies. They argue that large unconditional pricing errors would require implausibly large variation in betas and the market risk premium. Using short-window regressions to estimate conditional alphas and betas directly, they find that betas do vary over time, but not nearly enough to account for the anomalies.
